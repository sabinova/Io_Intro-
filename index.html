Projects

Machine Learning / AI

Elderly Fall Detection System

Developed a YOLOv8 computer vision system through the AI4ALL Ignite program to detect falls in images of older adults and support timely caregiver alerts. Fine-tuned on 1,784 custom-labeled images from Kaggle datasets (annotated with Roboflow), achieving ~94% precision and ~97% mAP@50 on validation. The project also examines class imbalance, annotation bias, and ethical considerations for AI in elderly healthcare.

Achieved 8/8 eval cases passed with an average quality score of 100.
